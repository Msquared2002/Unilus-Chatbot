UNILUS swimming pool rules
No pets. Do not swim alone. No littering, glass, bottles, food or drink. No running or rough play. Swim at your own risk. Shower before entering the pool. Use the stairs. The floor may be slippery; wear slippers. Remain alert. Management may refuse admission, eject or suspend people who disregard health and safety rules.
Observed on campus signage on 27 September 2026; operational details may change. Confirm current information with the facility.
