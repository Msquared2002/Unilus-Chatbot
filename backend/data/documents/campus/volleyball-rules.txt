UNILUS volleyball court rules
Use the court at your own risk. No food, glass or alcoholic beverages. No pets. No bicycles, rollerblades or skateboards. Proper footwear is required. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. Do not swing or pull on the net or abuse the net or equipment. No smoking. Court hours: 08:00 until dusk.
Observed on campus signage on 27 September 2026; operational details may change. Confirm current information with the facility.
