INTRODUCING ASTRIA DIGITAL LIBRARY

Dear Students,

Did you know? Have you heard? The University of Lusaka has subscribed to an electronic book (e-book) platform called ASTRIA Digital Library. This platform enables you, to access books in electronic format for your learning. These books can be accessed from any location on your mobile phones, tablets, laptops and desktops. Do not be left out of this wonderful experience.

STEPS FOR ACCESS

Step 1: Login credentials have been shared via your University of Lusaka student emails.

Step 2: The email also contains links directing you to where you can download the app depending on the gadget you are using.

Step 3: The email for credentials also contains links to User manuals and Video guides.

Step 4: Once you download Astria Digital Library, proceed to login using the credentials shared with you.

Step 5: Browse through Astria Digital Library to search for your prescribed and recommended books.
