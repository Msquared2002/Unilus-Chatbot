IMPORTANT REGISTRATION NOTICE TO ALL STUDENTS

Friday, 24th July 2026

Dear Students,

Please be advised that semester registration officially closes today, Friday, 24th July 2026, as classes are scheduled to commence on Monday, 27th July 2026.

Late Registration Period

Students who fail to register by the close of business today may take advantage of the late registration period, which will run from Monday, 27th July 2026 to Friday, 31st July 2026.

No further extension will be granted beyond this period, regardless of the mode of study.

Eligibility for University Activities

Kindly note that only fully registered students will be eligible to participate in the following University activities:

  • Class attendance
  • Assignment submissions
  • Clinical rotations
  • Quizzes, Tests, Mooting and Advocacy Skills
  • Mid-Semester Assessments
  • End-of-Semester Final Examinations

Attendance Without Registration

Students who are not registered are not permitted to attend classes.

Any student found attending classes without being duly registered will be subject to disciplinary action in accordance with the University's regulations.

Important Notice on Fee Payments

All tuition and other University payments must be made exclusively to the official University of Lusaka bank accounts.

The University does not request or accept payments through mobile money accounts.

The University wishes all students a successful and productive semester.
